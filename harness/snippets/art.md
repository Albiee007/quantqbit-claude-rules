Brand & story art checklist (web and mobile):
- Delegate by persona: brand-asset-creator (logo, tokens, splash, social images) feeds illustrator (story and scene art) with the palette.
- The owner chooses the logo direction and approves every image before it ships.
- Story art: one scene per real feature, fictional inclusive people, no text, logos or brands; one locked style bible per set.
- Export with export_art.py (budgets, AVIF + WebP, manifest merge); brand exports come from `brand/exports.json` (core set; extras only on request).
- Record prompts, provider and approvals in `out/story-art/<date>/plan.md`.
Load skill: story-art for illustrations; brand-assets for logos, tokens and brand images.
