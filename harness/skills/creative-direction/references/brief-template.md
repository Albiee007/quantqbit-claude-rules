# Brief template (`brand/BRIEF.md`)

Write this only when the project has no strategy document. If one exists (a brand book, a positioning doc, a pitch deck the docs link), cite it from `direction.json` instead. The brief is strategy; the direction is visual language. Don't repeat one in the other.

```markdown
# <Product> brief

Status: draft | confirmed by <owner> on <date> (<where>)

## Product
One sentence: what it does, for whom. (source: <path>)

## Audience
The first audience it must convince, and where they meet it (store search, a feed, a referral link).

## Positioning
The one thing it does better or differently, in the users' words.

## Personality
Three adjectives, each with what it rules out ("calm, so no exclamation marks or saturated reds").

## Competitors and clichés
Who the audience compares it with, and the category clichés to avoid (e.g. coins and upward arrows for finance).

## Where the brand must work
App icon, splash, store page, website, social, email, print, at which sizes and in which languages and scripts.

## Fixed
What must not change (a name, a colour, a mark), and why.

## Open questions
What the owner still has to decide.
```

Keep it under a page. Confirm it with the owner before the direction is drafted from it.
